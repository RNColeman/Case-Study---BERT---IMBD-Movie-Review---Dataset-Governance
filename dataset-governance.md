Dataset Governance — IMDB Movie Reviews

Data Quality 
- Reviews are user-generated and may contain noise.
- Language varies widely in tone, grammar, and structure.

Data Risks
- Potential for offensive language.
- Cultural bias in sentiment expression.
- Imbalanced vocabulary across genres.

Controls
- Preprocessing to remove harmful content.
- Tokenization normalization.
- Documentation of dataset limitations.
- Regular fairness audits on sentiment predictions.

Compliance
- Dataset contains no PII.
- No GDPR or HIPAA implications.
- Classified as Low-Risk under EU AI Act.

Residual Risks
Low, due to:
- Public nature of dataset
- Lack of sensitive attributes
- Minimal societal impact
